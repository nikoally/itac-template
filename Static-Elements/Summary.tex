\section{Summary}
In this assessment, several potential energy-saving projects have been identified, totaling \TotalRecSavings~per year. The total implementation cost for these recommendaitons is \TotalRecImplementation. Total electricity savings total to \TotalkWhSavings\ kWh of consumption and \TotalkWSavings\ kW of demand.  Fuel savings total to \TotalGasSavings\ MMBtu. 

Note that some recommendations require minimal payback periods, while others are larger capital projects that have longer but more significant returns. Some recommendations may be eligible for various cost-savings opportunities, including federal tax credits (30-40\% of project cost) and incentives offered by the utility providers. Moreover, this assessment also makes the plant eligible to apply for the upcoming Industrial Training and Assessment Center Implementation Grants offered by the Department of Energy\footnote{See \href{https://www.energywerx.org/itac}{\textcolor{blue}{link}} for more details}.


All utility incentives, as well as tax incentives and savings included, are estimates; all final incentive/tax saving amounts are pending utility and federal/state tax agencies for review and approval.
