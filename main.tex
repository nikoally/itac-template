\section*{PREFACE}
\phantomsection
This report was prepared as part of an assessment conducted under the Industrial Training and Assessment Centers (ITAC) program, a national initiative supported by the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) through the Office of Critical Minerals and Energy Innovation (CMEI). The ITAC program consists of 36 centers across the United States, each working with manufacturers to identify opportunities for energy innovation, waste reduction, and productivity improvements.

These one-day assessments are typically performed through site visits conducted by teams of engineering faculty and students, who collect operational information, evaluate energy systems, and identify potential improvement opportunities. The program also serves an important educational mission, providing hands-on training for students.

Recommendations in this report are based on observations, measurements, and discussions with facility personnel during the assessment visit. \textbf{As the assessment is generally conducted during a single-day site visit, the analysis presented in this report represents a snapshot of facility operations and may not capture every operational condition or opportunity for improvement.}

The ITAC team appreciates the cooperation and assistance provided by facility personnel during the assessment process. The insights and operational knowledge shared during the visit are critical to the development of meaningful recommendations. \vspace{20 pt}
